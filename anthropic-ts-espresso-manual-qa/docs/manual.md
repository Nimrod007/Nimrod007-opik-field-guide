# Brasa Lumina 7 Espresso Machine: Owner's Manual

Model BL-7 (dual boiler, 58 mm portafilter). Please read before first use.

## 1. Safety

Never operate the machine with the drip tray removed. Hot water can reach 96 C at the group head, so keep hands clear of the shower screen while the pump is running. The steam wand stays hot for 90 seconds after steaming; always purge it before touching the tip. Do not immerse the machine in water. Wipe the exterior with a damp cloth only.

## 2. First setup

Fill the water tank with fresh, cold, filtered water up to the MAX line (2.8 liters). Place the machine on a level surface with at least 10 cm of free space on each side. Switch the rear power switch on, then press the front Power button. The machine heats for about 8 minutes before the "Ready" light turns solid green. Before your first shot, run a flush of 500 ml of water through the group head and 200 ml through the steam wand.

## 3. Brewing a shot

Use 18 grams of ground coffee for the double basket and 9 grams for the single basket. Tamp with about 15 kg of pressure. The factory pump pressure is 9 bar and the factory brew temperature is 93 C. A good double shot takes between 25 and 32 seconds and yields roughly 36 grams in the cup. If the shot runs faster than 20 seconds, grind finer.

## 4. Steaming milk

Purge the wand for two seconds before and after every use. Use cold milk in a chilled pitcher, filled no more than halfway. Steam to 60 C to 65 C; above 70 C the milk scorches and loses sweetness. The wand has a cool-touch sleeve, but the tip itself is never cool.

## 5. Cleaning and maintenance

Backflush with the blind basket and one teaspoon of Brasa-approved cleaner every week. Rinse the portafilter and baskets after every session. Replace the water filter cartridge every 3 months, or after 150 liters, whichever comes first. Remove and wash the drip tray every day. Never use soap on the group head gasket.

## 6. Descaling

Descale every 4 months with the Brasa Descale Kit, or every 2 months if your water hardness is above 120 ppm. Do not use vinegar; it damages the boiler seals and voids the warranty. A descale cycle takes about 35 minutes. After descaling, run 2 liters of fresh water through the group head.

## 7. Troubleshooting

Error code E3 means the water tank is empty or not seated; refill it and push it firmly into place. Error code E7 means the brew boiler is overheating. Switch the machine off at the rear switch, wait 30 minutes, and switch it on again. If E7 returns, stop using the machine and contact support. If the pump is loud, the tank is probably nearly empty. If no steam comes out, clean the steam tip with the supplied pin.

## 8. Warranty

The BL-7 carries a 2-year limited warranty from the date of purchase. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship. It does not cover damage from descaling with vinegar, use of the machine without a water filter in hard-water areas, or commercial use. Keep your receipt; it is required for any claim. Register the machine within 30 days of purchase to extend the warranty to 3 years.
