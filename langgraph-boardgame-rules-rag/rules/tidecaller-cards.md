# Tidecaller

A fast card duel for exactly 2 players. Each player builds a tide of Wave cards and tries to drain the opponent's 20 Anchor points.

## Setup

Each player uses their own 30-card deck, shuffles it and draws 5 cards. Both players start with 20 Anchor points tracked on a dial. Flip a coin to see who goes first; that player skips their first draw.

## Your turn

A turn has four steps: Draw, Surge, Cast, Ebb. Draw 1 card. In Surge you gain 1 Surge crystal, to a maximum of 7 crystals. In Cast you may play any number of cards by paying their crystal cost. In Ebb you discard down to 6 cards in hand.

## Wave cards and Spell cards

Wave cards stay on the table and have an attack value. At the start of your Cast step, every Wave you control strikes: the opponent loses Anchor points equal to the total attack value of your Waves, minus 1 for each Reef card the opponent controls. Spell cards are played, resolved and then discarded immediately.

## Reef cards

Reef cards are defensive and stay on the table. Each Reef reduces incoming Wave damage by 1, and a player may control at most 3 Reefs. If a fourth Reef is played, the owner must discard one of their Reefs first. A Reef is never destroyed by Wave damage.

## Counter spells

A Counter spell may be played during the opponent's Cast step, in response to a card they have just played. It costs 2 crystals and cancels that card, which is discarded. You may counter only one card per opponent turn.

## Winning and running out of cards

You win when your opponent's Anchor total reaches 0. If a player must draw from an empty deck, they lose 3 Anchor points instead and the draw is skipped. If both players reach 0 Anchor in the same step, the player whose turn it is loses.
