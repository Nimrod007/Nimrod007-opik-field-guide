# Saltmarsh Traders

A 2-4 player trading game on a tidal coast. Players ship goods between harbours and race to collect 8 pearls.

## Setup

Each player takes one ship token, 5 coins and a hand of 3 cargo cards. Place the ship tokens on the Driftwood Quay harbour. Shuffle the tide deck and reveal the top card; it sets the starting tide. The youngest player goes first.

## Turn structure

A turn has three phases in order: Sail, Trade, Restock. In Sail you move your ship up to 3 harbours along the coast. In Trade you may sell or buy cargo at the harbour you are in. In Restock you draw cargo cards until you hold 3 again. You may not skip a phase, but you may do nothing in it.

## The tide track

At the end of every round, the first player flips the top card of the tide deck. High tide lets ships move 1 extra harbour. Low tide means ships may not enter Shallows harbours (Mudflat Cove and Gull Reach). Storm tide ends sailing for the round for any ship not currently in a harbour; those ships lose 1 cargo card of their owner's choice.

## Selling cargo

Selling a cargo card pays the price printed at the current harbour. You may sell up to 2 cards of the same type per turn. If you sell 2 cards of the same type, the second one pays 1 coin less. Contract cards are sold only at the harbour named on them and pay double.

## Pearls and winning

Pearls are bought at the Pearl Exchange in Driftwood Quay for 6 coins each. You may buy only 1 pearl per turn. The first player to own 8 pearls wins at the end of that round, so every player gets an equal number of turns. If two players tie on pearls, the one with more coins wins.

## Piracy

If your ship ends Sail in the same harbour as an opponent's ship, you may declare piracy once per turn. Roll the tide die: on 4-6 you take one cargo card at random from that player's hand; on 1-3 you pay them 1 coin. A ship in Driftwood Quay cannot be raided.
