# Emberfall Keep

A cooperative dungeon game for 1-4 heroes. Together you descend five floors of a burning keep and must extinguish the Heart Flame on floor five before the Ash Clock runs out.

## Setup

Each hero picks a class card and takes 12 health tokens and 2 ember dice. Place the Ash Clock marker on space 20. Build floor one from its 6 room tiles and place the party in the Gatehouse. Shuffle the monster deck and the relic deck separately.

## Hero turn

On your turn you have 3 action points. Spend 1 to move to an adjacent room, 1 to search a room for a relic, 2 to attack, or 1 to help a hero in your room (they heal 2 health). You may take the same action more than once. Unspent action points are lost.

## Combat

To attack, roll all of your ember dice and add the results. If the total equals or beats the monster's defence, it takes 1 wound. Rolling a double on your ember dice is a Flare: the attack deals 2 wounds instead of 1. Monsters strike back after every attack you make that does not defeat them, dealing the damage printed on their card to you.

## The Ash Clock

At the end of each full round of hero turns, move the Ash Clock marker down 1 space. When it reaches 10, draw a monster from the deck and place it in the Gatehouse of the current floor. When it reaches 0, the keep collapses and every hero loses.

## Relics

A relic is kept face up in front of you. A hero may carry at most 3 relics. Trading a relic to a hero in the same room costs 1 action point. A Quenching Flask relic lets you move the Ash Clock marker back up 3 spaces, once, and is then discarded.

## Falling and revival

A hero at 0 health falls and is laid on their side. A fallen hero skips their turns. Another hero in the same room can revive them by spending 2 action points; the revived hero returns with 4 health. If all heroes are fallen at the same time, the game is lost.
