# Band Plans of the Larkspur Valley Amateur Radio Club

## 2-meter band layout
The club repeater LV-2 listens on 146.610 MHz and transmits on 146.010 MHz, a 600 kHz offset downward, with a 88.5 Hz access tone. Simplex calling is on 146.520 MHz. Keep simplex conversations short and move off the calling frequency to 146.490 or 146.550 MHz after the first exchange.

## 70-centimeter band layout
The club repeater LV-7 transmits on 444.325 MHz and listens on 449.325 MHz, a 5 MHz offset upward, with a 107.2 Hz access tone. LV-7 is linked to LV-2 only during scheduled nets. The 70-centimeter simplex calling frequency is 446.000 MHz.

## 10-meter band layout
Larkspur members use 28.300 to 28.500 MHz for single sideband voice. The beacon sub-band is 28.200 to 28.300 MHz: never transmit voice there. When the band is open, check the beacon sub-band first to see whether propagation reaches the valley.

## Power limits
Club repeaters run 25 watts. Members on club simplex frequencies use no more than 50 watts, and drop to 5 watts handheld power inside the clubhouse to avoid overloading the shared receive preamplifier.
