# Antenna Basics

## Quarter-wave ground plane
For the 2-meter band, a quarter-wave ground plane vertical has a radiator about 48.5 centimeters long, with four radials sloped down at roughly 135 degrees. The sloped radials bring the feed impedance close to 50 ohms. The club mast at the clubhouse carries one for LV-2.

## Standing wave ratio
Check SWR before every long transmission. An SWR below 1.5 to 1 is excellent, up to 2 to 1 is acceptable, and anything above 3 to 1 means you must stop transmitting and inspect the coax, connectors, and antenna. Never key the radio into a disconnected antenna.

## Coax and connectors
The club standard is RG-8X coax for runs under 15 meters and LMR-400 beyond that. Use PL-259 connectors on HF and N-type connectors on 70 centimeters. Seal every outdoor connector with self-fusing tape followed by vinyl tape.

## Lightning safety
Disconnect and ground antenna feed lines at the entry panel when thunderstorms are forecast. Do not operate during a storm, and keep the grounding rod bonded to the mast and to the station ground with heavy braid.
