# Emergency Net Etiquette

## Activating the net
The Larkspur Emergency Net is activated by the net manager or the county dispatcher. The net meets on the LV-2 repeater at 146.010 MHz. If LV-2 is down, move to simplex on 146.520 MHz. Check-in begins as soon as the net control station announces "the net is open".

## Checking in
Wait until net control asks for check-ins, then say only your call sign using phonetics, once. Do not add your location or availability unless asked. Stations with emergency traffic say "priority" before their call sign, and net control answers them first.

## Net control duties
Net control keeps a roster of every checked-in station and its capability, such as shelter, medical, or relay. Net control gives a roll call every 30 minutes and may close the net only after dispatch confirms that service is restored.

## Plain language and brevity
During an activation, speak in plain language: do not use Q-codes or ten-codes. Repeat any address, name, or number twice. Messages relayed for served agencies are written on the green message pad and read back to the sender before being sent.
