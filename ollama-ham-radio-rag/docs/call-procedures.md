# Call Procedures

## Making a general call
To start a conversation on a repeater, say "Larkspur Valley, this is" followed by your call sign spoken with the club phonetic alphabet. Do not say "CQ" on a repeater; CQ is for simplex and HF calling. Wait two seconds between overs so that other members can break in.

## Phonetic alphabet
Larkspur uses standard phonetics, except that members say "Larkspur" for the letter L during nets, so that it is not confused with "Lima" on a noisy link. Always give your call sign at the end of a conversation and at least every ten minutes.

## Breaking in
To join a conversation, say your call sign once between overs. Never say "break, break" unless you have an emergency or priority traffic. If you hear a station calling you, answer with their call sign, then yours, then "go ahead".

## Logging contacts
Every contact is entered in the station log with the date in UTC, frequency, call signs, signal report, and a one-line note. Logs are kept for two years. The club uses the signal report format of readability then strength, for example "five nine".
