# Water Parameters

## Temperature
Tropical community fish such as tetras, rasboras and corydoras are comfortable between 24 and 27 C (75 to 81 F). Goldfish and white cloud mountain minnows prefer cooler water, 18 to 22 C (64 to 72 F). Keep the heater set to a fixed value and change it by no more than 1 C per hour, because sudden swings stress fish and invite white spot.

## pH and hardness
Most soft-water species from the Ruvani river basin do best at pH 6.0 to 7.0 and general hardness (GH) of 3 to 8 dGH. Livebearers such as guppies and platies prefer pH 7.2 to 8.2 and GH of 10 to 18 dGH. Do not chase a perfect pH with chemicals; a stable value inside the species range is safer than a corrected one that bounces.

## Nitrogen cycle
A healthy, cycled tank shows 0 ppm ammonia, 0 ppm nitrite and under 20 ppm nitrate. Ammonia or nitrite above 0.25 ppm is an emergency: do a 50 percent water change with dechlorinated water at matching temperature, stop feeding for a day, and retest after six hours. A new tank takes four to six weeks to cycle; add fish slowly, two or three small ones per week.

## Water changes
Change 25 percent of the water weekly in a stocked tank. Always treat tap water with a dechlorinator, and vacuum the gravel lightly rather than stripping it, so the bacteria colony living in the filter and substrate survives.
